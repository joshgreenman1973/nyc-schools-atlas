2025–2026

Below is a directory of the 370 charter schools that are currently operating or have been approved but are not yet in operation (237 SUNY-authorized, 95 Board of Regents-authorized, 36 NYCDoE-authorized, and 2 Buffalo BoE-authorized) as of May 2025.

The directory offers information about each school, recent annual reports and audited financial statements, and a link to the school's website. Charter schools authorized by the New York City Department of Education and the State University of New York also have links to information that is maintained by their authorizers.
