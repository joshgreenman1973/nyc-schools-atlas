# New York City school time, 2026–27

The "School day" section of the NYC Schools Atlas: scheduled hours in school for New York City public school students in 2026–27 compared with the national average, how the year's weeks are broken up by grade band, a peer and charter comparison, and a "build your own school year" calculator.

**Live:** https://joshgreenman1973.github.io/nyc-schools-atlas/school-day/

(An older standalone copy at https://joshgreenman1973.github.io/nyc-school-time/ predates the Oct. 7, 2026, corrections.)

## Files

- `index.html` — the page: 110-hour gap chart, week grid by grade band (computed from the official calendar), peer and charter comparison, calculator and calendar summary
- `methodology.html` — formulas, sources, caveats and the corrections log
- `data.json` — figures, calendar events, peer schedules, sources and quotes
- `data.csv` — peer and charter comparison flattened to CSV

## Sources

See `methodology.html` for the full list. Primary sources: the NYC Public Schools 2026–27 and 2025–26 calendars; Kraft and Novicoff, "Time for School" (Education Next, Winter 2025); Chalkbeat reporting (Feb. 27 and April 21, 2026; Sept. 9, 2025); 8 NYCRR 175.5 and Education Law § 3604; the United Federation of Teachers contract; district calendars and bell schedules; charter networks' 2026–27 calendars and family handbooks.

## Caveats

- Scheduled hours run bell to bell and include lunch and recess. New York State's "instructional hours" exclude lunch and recess, so these are not the state's measure.
- The national comparison figure (1,231 hours) is from 2017–18 survey data.
- The K–12 total assumes the 2026–27 schedule holds for 13 years and perfect attendance.
- Charter schedules vary by school and grade; the chart shows ranges where they do.
