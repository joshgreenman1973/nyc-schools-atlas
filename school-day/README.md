# New York City instructional time, 2026–27

Standalone, self-contained data project on the New York City public school instructional time gap, with calendar fragmentation analysis and a "build your own school year" calculator.

**Live:** https://joshgreenman1973.github.io/nyc-school-time/

## Files

- `index.html` — Headline infographic: 129-hour gap chart, peer/charter comparison chart, 2026–27 calendar fragmentation grid, and an interactive "build your own school year" calculator
- `methodology.html` — Full methodology, formulas, caveats, and per-system sources
- `data.json` — Structured source data (figures, calendar events, sources, quotes)
- `data.csv` — Peer/charter comparison flattened to CSV

## Sources

See `methodology.html` for the complete source list. Primary sources: official New York City Public Schools 2026–27 calendar; Kraft, Goldstein & Lyon, *Time for School* (Education Next, 2025); Chalkbeat reporting; Citizens Budget Commission; UFT contract; published bell schedules and academic calendars from peer districts and charter networks.

## Caveats

- Bell-to-bell scheduled hours including lunch/recess — closest cross-jurisdiction comparable measure, but not identical to what state regulators count.
- The 1.5-year cumulative K–12 gap is illustrative; assumes constant annual gap and perfect attendance.
- Charter networks chose longer days; comparison is descriptive, not normative.
